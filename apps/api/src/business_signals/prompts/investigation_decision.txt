Decide whether another internal investigation is needed, a targeted external check would meaningfully discriminate a plausible external hypothesis, or the available evidence is sufficient to finish. Choose external only after internal evidence has confirmed the business change and ruled out or weakened relevant internal explanations. If the question concerns a specific place and historical period, a relevant external hypothesis remains plausible, and no internal cause has direct evidence, choose a targeted external check rather than finish. A broad pattern of lower sales does not by itself prove a recording problem. Never use external research as a broad search or to make up for missing connected business data.
